# Edgewater Insurance Solutions — Brand Guidelines

**Version 1.0 · March 2026**

---

## Brand Overview

Edgewater Insurance Solutions is a trusted partner to California's firefighters, police officers, and first responders. For over 17 years, the firm has served 10,000+ families across the San Francisco Bay Area and throughout the state.

**Core Pillars:** Trust & Reliability · Stewardship · Community First

**Tagline:** "A Trusted Partner to Those Who Protect and Serve"

---

## Logo

The logo consists of a compass rose mark paired with the EDGEWATER wordmark and "INSURANCE SOLUTIONS" subtitle.

- **Primary:** White logo on navy backgrounds
- **Alternate:** Navy logo on light/cream backgrounds
- **Clear space:** Minimum padding equal to the height of the "E" on all sides
- **Minimum size:** 1.5 in (print) / 120px (digital)
- **Favicon:** Compass mark may be used independently

**Don'ts:** No stretching, skewing, recoloring outside palette, busy backgrounds without overlay, drop shadows, or separating the mark from the wordmark.

## Typography

### Display / Headings — Poppins

Used for hero text, page headings, section titles, and stat callouts. Weights: SemiBold, Bold, ExtraBold.

### Body / UI — DM Sans

Used for body copy, navigation, buttons, captions, and form labels. Weights: Regular, Medium, Bold.

### Type Scale

| Level            | Font              | Size         | Notes                    |
|------------------|-------------------|--------------|--------------------------|
| H1 — Hero        | Poppins Bold      | 40–56px      | Uppercase for hero text  |
| H2 — Section     | Poppins Bold      | 28–36px      | Title case               |
| H3 — Subsection  | Poppins SemiBold  | 20–24px      |                          |
| Body             | DM Sans Regular   | 16–18px      | 1.6 line-height          |
| Caption / Label  | DM Sans Medium    | 12–14px      | Uppercase, tracked        |

## Photography & Imagery

**Hero Photography:** Real first responders in authentic settings. Warm, slightly desaturated grade. Apply a navy overlay (`#0A2640` at 60–70% opacity) for text legibility.

**Portraits:** Three-column grid (Firefighters, Police, Municipal). Eye contact or candid engagement. Tight crops with environmental context. Consistent warm color treatment.

**Lifestyle & Family:** Off-duty moments — families, pets, home life. Bright, airy, warm cream tones. Candid and editorial. No stock-photo stiffness.

---

## Voice & Tone

Edgewater speaks like a trusted colleague — knowledgeable but never condescending, direct but always warm.

- **Confident, not pushy** — State value clearly without pressure
- **Warm, not casual** — Professional but approachable
- **Direct, not cold** — Clear language, short sentences, no jargon
- **Empathetic, not patronizing** — Honor the risks without talking down

### Signature Phrases

- "A Trusted Partner to Those Who Protect and Serve" — Primary tagline
- "Listen First. Prepare Together." — Service philosophy
- "You Protect the Community. Let Us Help You Protect Your Own." — Emotional close

## UI Patterns

**Primary CTA:** Coral (`#F07850`) fill, white text, pill-shaped (full border-radius). Example: `TALK TO US: (415) 876-5490`

**Secondary CTA:** Transparent or navy fill with white border, white text, pill-shaped. Example: `SCHEDULE A CALL`

**Section Eyebrow:** Coral, uppercase, tracked letter-spacing (e.g., `WHAT WE DO`)

**Stat Callouts:** Coral, bold, large (e.g., `10.2k`) followed by descriptive body text in navy/gray.

**Backgrounds:** Alternate between white and warm cream sections. Navy for hero and footer areas.
