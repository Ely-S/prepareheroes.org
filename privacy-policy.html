Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1/2026

At Edgewater Insurance Solutions LLC, we respect your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle the information you provide through our insurance qualification tools and website.

Information Collection & Purpose

We collect information you voluntarily provide through our insurance quizzes, such as demographic data, health or lifestyle details, and contact information. This data is used exclusively to:

  • Generate accurate insurance quotes.
  • Match you with the most appropriate insurance carriers.
  • Verify eligibility for specific coverage options.

Our "No-Sale" Guarantee

Your trust is our priority. Edgewater Insurance Solutions LLC does not sell, rent, or lease your quiz responses or personal data to third-party marketing firms. We only share your information with licensed insurance providers or essential service partners for the sole purpose of securing the insurance products you have requested.

We may share your information:

  • With service providers to help us operate our business
  • For legal reasons, such as compliance with laws or responding to lawful requests
  • With consent from the user

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your sensitive information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Your Rights

You may request to review, update, or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us at planning@edgewaterins.com.
